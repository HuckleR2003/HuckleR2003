## My story

Nine months in a warehouse in the Netherlands. 15 km a day, scanning barcodes. Coding every night on a
laptop that hits 94°C, because I was afraid of disappearing.

Lost the job on 22 December, three days before Christmas, with the apartment attached. Started rebuild #4
that night. Shipped the first .exe three weeks later.

A year on: twelve dead projects behind it, Google's AI programme finished, and the last line of a roadmap I
never believed I would reach: **live on the Microsoft Store.**

Now I drive a taxi ten to twelve hours a day and build at night. In August that turned into a game.
